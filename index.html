IFwalker

IFwalker は、「もしも〇〇だったら」の世界を一人称で描く、選択型のショートドラマを制作・投稿している個人のクリエイター活動です。

IFwalker is an individual creator project that makes short first-person "what if" drama videos. The IFwalker app is a private tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos as drafts / private videos and to read the statistics of the owner's own accounts.
